About this index. Build: 1,000 resources across 16 categories (1,000 unique web addresses; some providers host several resources, 51 flagged as AI tools). A directory of universities, funders, labs, societies, libraries, journals, repositories, methods software and AI tools for study and research. Every entry has a name, a description and a clickable link to the provider's own site. Build: 1,000 resources across 16 categories (51 flagged as AI tools). Filtering runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Category boundaries are a guide, not a ruling. Categories follow each resource's stated focus, so a university press, a lab or a platform that spans fields could equally sit in a neighbouring section. Use the search box and the category drop-down together to narrow things down.

Link reliability. Not every page could be opened from the build environment (outbound requests were blocked there), so a small number of URLs may have moved, be paywalled or be region-restricted. Export the CSV and run a link checker, then replace any dead entries with “+ Add resource”.

Access & pricing. Free / freemium / paid / open-access labels are approximate: institutional subscriptions, regional availability, student discounts and author-pays open access change often. Many “paid” journals are readable free through a library, and many “free” datasets require a login. Always confirm on the provider's site.
