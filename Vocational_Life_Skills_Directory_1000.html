About this index. A directory of websites, public programmes, training providers, unions, non-profits, apps and AI tools for vocational and everyday life skills: career exploration, skills training and online courses, apprenticeships and skilled trades, job search and career change, workplace and everyday skills, gig work and freelancing, money and benefits, home and family, health and safety, community and volunteering, and AI assistants. Every entry links straight to the source. Build: 1000 resources across 11 categories (117 flagged as AI tools). Filtering runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Placement is a guide, not a ruling. Categories follow each resource's stated focus, so an adult-literacy programme or a public benefit could equally sit in a neighbouring section. Use the search box and the category drop-down together to narrow things down. “Level” describes who the resource is mainly aimed at, not a formal qualification.

Rules, pay rates and eligibility change. Government benefit amounts, tax thresholds, minimum wages, apprenticeship rules and programme availability differ by state and by year. Always confirm on the official page before applying or signing a contract. Nothing here is legal, tax, financial or medical advice.

Link reliability. Not every page could be opened from the build environment (outbound requests were blocked there), so a small number of URLs may have moved, be paywalled or be region-restricted. Export the CSV and run a link checker, then replace any dead entries with “+ Add resource”.

User-added resources are stored only in this browser tab and disappear when you reload the page.
