About this index. A directory of music schools and conservatoires, music-industry and rights organisations, music media and charts, festivals, streaming and distribution services, artist rights and royalty administration, instruments, studio and live-sound equipment, recording and production software, podcasting and game audio, and the AI music and audio tools used to write, generate, mix, master and separate tracks. Every entry links straight to the source. Build: 1,000 resources across 5 categories (62 flagged as AI tools). Filtering runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Placement is a guide, not a ruling. A resource that spans fields (say, emotional intelligence and coaching) could equally sit in a neighbouring section. Use the search box and the category drop-down together to narrow things down.

Link reliability. Not every page could be opened from the build environment (outbound requests were blocked there), so some URLs may have moved, be paywalled or be region-restricted. Export the CSV and run a link checker, then replace any dead entries with “+ Add resource”.

Access & pricing. Free / freemium / paid labels are approximate: university subscriptions, student discounts, regional availability and corporate licences change often. Many “paid” journals are readable free through a library, and many “free” tools require an account. Programme fees and credential rules change yearly — always confirm on the provider's site.
