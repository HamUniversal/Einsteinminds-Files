About the data. Entries were assembled from first-hand project knowledge and cross-checked against vendor product pages, standards-body catalogues, government and agency sites, package indexes, cooler stuff like awesome-lists, and university course listings. A single product can carry several cards — a CAD suite and its built-in CAM or AI assistant, for example — so per-category counts overlap.

Accuracy limits. Pricing and free tiers change often; treat the access labels as a rough guide and confirm with the provider before committing. Regional availability, site licences, export controls and student discounts are not modelled, and a minority of hosts block automated requests while serving browsers normally.

Adding entries. Use “+ Add resource”. Additions persist in localStorage on this machine only, are merged on load, and can be cleared from the browser’s site settings. Export CSV gives you a flat file of whatever is currently filtered.
