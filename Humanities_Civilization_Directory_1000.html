About the data. Entries were assembled from first-hand project knowledge and cross-checked against institutional homepages, national library and archive catalogues, university department listings, publisher platforms, professional-society directories and open-data portals. A single institution can carry several cards — a national library and its digitised manuscript portal, for example — so per-category counts overlap slightly.

Accuracy limits. Pricing and free tiers change often; treat the access labels as a rough guide and confirm with the provider before committing. Regional availability, institutional subscriptions, licensing and student discounts are not modelled, and a minority of hosts block automated requests while serving browsers normally. Where a project moved (for example the Perseus library now reads through the Scaife Viewer), the card points at the address its maintainers currently publish.

Adding entries. Use “+ Add resource”. Additions persist in localStorage on this machine only, are merged on load, and can be cleared from the browser’s site settings. Export CSV gives you a flat file of whatever is currently filtered.
