About this index. A directory of websites, datasets, associations, journals and tools for pregnancy, birth and the early years. Every entry has a name, a description and a clickable link to the provider's own site. Build: 1,000 resources across 11 categories ((89 flagged as AI tools)). Filtering runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Placement is a guide, not a ruling. Categories follow each resource's stated focus, so a journal or dataset that spans fields (say, parenting and child health) could equally sit in a neighbouring section. Use the search box and the category drop-down together to narrow things down.

Link reliability. Not every page could be opened from the build environment (outbound requests were blocked there), so a small number of URLs may have moved, be paywalled or be region-restricted. Export the CSV and run a link checker, then replace any dead entries with “+ Add resource”.

Access & pricing. Free / freemium / paid / open-access labels are approximate: institutional subscriptions, regional availability, student discounts and author-pays open access change often. Many “paid” journals are readable free through a library, and many “free” datasets require a login. Always confirm on the provider's site.
