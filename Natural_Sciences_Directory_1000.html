About the data. Entries were assembled from first-hand project knowledge and cross-checked against institutional, society and project pages, mission and agency data catalogues, CRAN/Bioconda-style package indexes and university course catalogues. Categories overlap by design — the EMBL-EBI AlphaFold database appears under both AI for Science and Open Data, for example — so per-category counts sum to more than the entry total.

Accuracy limits. Pricing and free tiers change often; treat the access labels as a rough guide and confirm on the provider's site before committing. Regional availability, institutional licences and student discounts are not modelled. A minority of hosts block automated requests but serve browsers normally.

Adding entries. Use “+ Add resource”. Additions persist in localStorage on this machine only, are merged on load, and can be cleared from the browser's site settings. Export CSV gives you a flat file of whatever is currently filtered.
